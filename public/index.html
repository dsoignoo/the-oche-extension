THE WHOLE CHECKOUT PATH

Which finish should I leave?

All double finishes and bull · expected darts to finish from a fresh visit, misses followed across later visits. Lower is better.

Expected darts counts every throw from a fresh three-dart visit at the listed score, then follows your recorded misses across later visits. Each branch is weighted by how often your history actually reaches it. For example, 36 left · D18 can still be a good leave despite a weak D9 if an inside single rarely sends you to 18 left · D9.
